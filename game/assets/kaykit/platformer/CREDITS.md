# KayKit Platformer Pack

`lever_floor_base_red` and `button_base_red`, from the **KayKit Platformer Pack
1.0 (FREE tier)** by Kay Lousberg, `kaylousberg.itch.io`. **CC0**: free for
personal and commercial use, attribution not required. Credit is given anyway,
as it is for the Dungeon Pack and the Adventurers.

Two models out of 370, copied rather than vendored whole: the rest of the pack
is bright platformer blockwork that would not sit beside a stone dungeon, and an
asset directory is a promise that everything in it is used.

The pack's paid EXTRA tier carries the things this project has had to fake --
sawblades, hammer traps, chains, conveyor belts. `SweepHazard` currently swings
a `axe_1handed` from the Adventurers pack because the free tier has no blade.

The unmodified download is in `assets_src/`, which is gitignored.
